# Word of the Day

## loquacious

### Meaning
tending to talk a great deal; very talkative

### Example
During the road trip, Maya became loquacious, narrating every detail of the scenery and sharing stories from her childhood.

### Difficulty
Upper-intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ləˈkweɪʃəs/

### Synonymn
chatty, garrulous, voluble, verbose

### Antonymn
reticent, taciturn, reserved, silent
