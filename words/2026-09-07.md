# Word of the Day

## obstinate

### Meaning
Stubbornly refusing to change one's opinion or chosen course of action, despite attempts to persuade one otherwise.

### Example
Even after the manager explained the benefits of the new software, Mark stayed obstinate and continued using the outdated system.

### Difficulty
Advanced

### Pronunciation
/ˈɒb.stɪ.nət/

### Synonym
stubborn
