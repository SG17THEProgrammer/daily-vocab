# Word of the Day

## ebullient

### Meaning
Cheerful and full of energy; overflowing with enthusiasm.

### Example
After receiving the good news, Maya was ebullient, bouncing around the office and sharing her excitement with everyone.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ɪˈbʌl.i.ənt/

### Synonymn
enthusiastic, exuberant, lively, buoyant

### Antonymn
subdued, apathetic, morose, lethargic
