# Word of the Day

## benevolent

### Meaning
Showing kindness, generosity, and a desire to help others.

### Example
The benevolent neighbor always helped the elderly residents with their groceries.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
bə-NEV-uh-lənt

### Synonymn
charitable, kind, compassionate, humane

### Antonymn
malevolent, selfish, unkind, cruel
