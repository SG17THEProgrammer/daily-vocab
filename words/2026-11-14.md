# Word of the Day

## capricious

### Meaning
Given to sudden and unaccountable changes of mood or behavior; unpredictable.

### Example
My brother is so capricious about his weekend plans that we never know if he'll want to hike, watch a movie, or just stay home until the last minute.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/kəˈprɪʃəs/

### Synonymn
unpredictable, whimsical, fickle, erratic

### Antonymn
stable, consistent, predictable, steadfast
