# Word of the Day

## veracious

### Meaning
Speaking or representing the truth; truthful and accurate.

### Example
She gave a veracious account of what happened at the meeting, and everyone appreciated her honesty.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
vuh-RAY-shuhs

### Synonymn
truthful, honest, sincere, faithful

### Antonymn
deceitful, dishonest, untruthful, false
