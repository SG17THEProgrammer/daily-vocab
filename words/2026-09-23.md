# Word of the Day

## intransigent

### Meaning
Unwilling or refusing to change one's views or to agree about something; stubbornly inflexible.

### Example
Even after hours of negotiation, the union remained intransigent, refusing to accept any compromise on wages.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
in-tran-ˈsi-jənt

### Synonym
uncompromising
