# Word of the Day

## sagacious

### Meaning
Having keen mental discernment and good judgment; wise and shrewd.

### Example
When we were debating the project timeline, Maya's sagacious comments helped us avoid a costly mistake.

### Difficulty
Advanced

### Pronunciation
səˈɡeɪʃəs

### Synonymn
wise, shrewd, perceptive, astute

### Antonymn
foolish, ignorant, unwise, naïve
