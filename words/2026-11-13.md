# Word of the Day

## cathartic

### Meaning
Providing psychological relief through the open expression of strong emotions; causing a feeling of purification or release.

### Example
After she finally talked about the breakup, she felt a cathartic sense of calm that she'd been missing for weeks.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/kəˈθɑːr.tɪk/

### Synonymn
purifying, cleansing, therapeutic, relieving

### Antonymn
distressing, traumatic, aggravating, stressful
