# Word of the Day

## innovative

### Meaning
Introducing new ideas; original and creative in thinking or methods.

### Example
The team praised Maya for her innovative approach to streamlining the workflow, which cut processing time by half.

### Difficulty
Upper-intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ɪˈnɒvətɪv/

### Synonymn
creative, inventive, original, groundbreaking

### Antonymn
conventional, traditional, unoriginal, derivative
