# Word of the Day

## equanimity

### Meaning
Mental calmness, composure, and evenness of temper, especially in a difficult situation.

### Example
Even when the deadline was moved up, she handled the pressure with remarkable equanimity.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
/ˌiːkwəˈnɪmɪti/

### Synonymn
composure, calmness, tranquility, poise

### Antonymn
anxiety, agitation, restlessness, unease
