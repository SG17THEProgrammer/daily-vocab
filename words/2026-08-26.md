# Word of the Day

## magnanimous

### Meaning
showing a generous spirit, especially towards a rival or someone less powerful; forgiving and unselfish.

### Example
Even after winning the debate, she was magnanimous, praising her opponent's points and thanking the audience for their support.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
/mæɡˈnænɪməs/

### Synonym
generous
