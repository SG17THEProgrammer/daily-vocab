# Word of the Day

## quintessential

### Meaning
Representing the most perfect or typical example of a quality or class.

### Example
She is the quintessential teacher—always patient, well-prepared, and genuinely caring about her students.

### Difficulty
Advanced

### Pronunciation
/ˌkwɪn.tɪˈsen.ʃəl/

### Synonym
archetypal
