# Word of the Day

## obsequious

### Meaning
excessively eager to please or obey; overly submissive

### Example
He was so obsequious that he laughed at the boss's jokes even when they were clearly inappropriate.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
/əbˈsiːkwiəs/

### Synonymn
subservient, servile, fawning, ingratiating

### Antonymn
assertive, independent, defiant, self-assured
