# Word of the Day

## meritocratic

### Meaning
relating to a system in which people are given power or rewards based on their abilities and achievements

### Example
The company prides itself on being a meritocratic organization, where promotions are based solely on individual performance

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
/ˌmɛrɪtəkrəˈtɪk/

### Synonym
egalitarian
