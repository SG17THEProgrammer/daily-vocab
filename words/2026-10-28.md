# Word of the Day

## versatile

### Meaning
Having many different skills, abilities, or uses; able to adapt to various tasks or situations.

### Example
She’s so versatile—one minute she’s leading the marketing team, the next she’s coding a new feature for the app.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ˈvɜːrsətaɪl/

### Synonymn
adaptable, flexible, multifaceted, resourceful

### Antonymn
inflexible, rigid, single-purpose, narrow-minded
