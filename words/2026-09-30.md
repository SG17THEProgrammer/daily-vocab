# Word of the Day

## capricious

### Meaning
Characterized by sudden, unpredictable changes in mood or behavior; whimsical.

### Example
She was so capricious that one minute she wanted to stay in, and the next she booked a last‑minute flight to the beach.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ˈkæprɪʃəs/

### Synonym
whimsical
