# Word of the Day

## camaraderie

### Meaning
A feeling of mutual trust, friendship, and goodwill among people who spend a lot of time together.

### Example
After pulling the all‑night shift together, there was a real sense of camaraderie among the nurses.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ˌkæməˈrɑːdəri/

### Synonym
fellowship
