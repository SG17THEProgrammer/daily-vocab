# Word of the Day

## pugnacious

### Meaning
Eager or quick to argue, quarrel, or fight; inclined to be combative.

### Example
Even though he's usually laid‑back, his pugnacious side shows up whenever someone questions his favorite sports team.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
ˈpʌɡ.neɪ.ʃəs

### Synonym
combative
