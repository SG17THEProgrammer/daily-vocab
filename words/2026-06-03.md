# Word of the Day

## hibernate

### Meaning
to spend the winter months in a state of inactivity and reduced metabolism, or to be in a state of inactivity or dormancy

### Example
The bear will hibernate in its cave during the long, cold winter, surviving on its stored fat reserves

### Difficulty
medium

### Pronunciation
/haɪˈbɜːneɪt/

### Synonym
dormant
