# Word of the Day

## inscrutable

### Meaning
Difficult to understand or interpret; mysterious and unreadable.

### Example
His expression was inscrutable, so I couldn't tell whether he was pleased or annoyed by the news.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ɪnˈskruːtəbl/

### Synonymn
enigmatic, impenetrable, unfathomable, mysterious

### Antonymn
clear, understandable, transparent, obvious
