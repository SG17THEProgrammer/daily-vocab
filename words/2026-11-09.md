# Word of the Day

## aberrant

### Meaning
Deviating from the usual or expected course; abnormal or atypical.

### Example
The scientist's aberrant results puzzled the entire research team, prompting them to double‑check the equipment.

### Difficulty
Advanced

### Pronunciation
uh-BER-uhnt

### Synonymn
deviant, anomalous, atypical, irregular

### Antonymn
normal, typical, conventional, standard
