# Word of the Day

## perfunctory

### Meaning
Carried out with minimal effort or reflection; hasty and superficial.

### Example
She gave a perfunctory smile and signed the papers without really reading them.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
pər-ˈfəŋk-tə-rē

### Synonymn
cursory, superficial, hasty, token

### Antonymn
thorough, meticulous, detailed, careful, exhaustive
