# Word of the Day

## pensive

### Meaning
deeply or seriously thoughtful, often with a hint of sadness or reflection.

### Example
She gave a pensive smile as she recalled the summer they spent together.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ˈpɛn.sɪv/

### Synonymn
thoughtful, reflective, contemplative, introspective

### Antonymn
indifferent, thoughtless, careless, unreflective
