# Word of the Day

## industrious

### Meaning
Diligently hardworking; showing steady effort and perseverance in tasks.

### Example
Jake is so industrious that he often finishes his projects days before the deadline, impressing everyone on the team.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ɪnˈdʌstriəs/

### Synonymn
hardworking, diligent, assiduous, tireless

### Antonymn
lazy, idle, indolent, slothful
