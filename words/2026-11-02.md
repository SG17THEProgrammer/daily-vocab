# Word of the Day

## capricious

### Meaning
Given to sudden and unaccountable changes of mood or behavior; unpredictable.

### Example
My brother is so capricious that one minute he'll want pizza and the next he'll insist on sushi, leaving us all guessing what to order.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
/kəˈprɪʃəs/

### Synonymn
whimsical, unpredictable, impulsive, erratic

### Antonymn
consistent, steady, predictable, stable
