# Word of the Day

## vexatious

### Meaning
Causing annoyance, frustration, or worry; burdensome and irritating.

### Example
The vexatious glitches in the app made it impossible for us to finish the project on time.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
ˈvɛkˌseɪʃəs

### Synonymn
annoying, irritating, aggravating, exasperating

### Antonymn
pleasant, soothing, agreeable, enjoyable
