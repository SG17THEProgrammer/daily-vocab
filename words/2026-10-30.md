# Word of the Day

## fortuitous

### Meaning
occurring by chance, often in a lucky or beneficial way

### Example
It was fortuitous that I found a ten-dollar bill on the sidewalk just before the bus left.

### Difficulty
upper-intermediate

### Pronunciation
/fɔːrˈtjuːɪtəs/

### Synonymn
accidental, inadvertent, serendipitous, unplanned

### Antonymn
intentional, deliberate, planned, purposeful
