# Word of the Day

## sagacious

### Meaning
Having or showing keen mental discernment and good judgment; wise.

### Example
When the project started to go off track, Maya’s sagacious advice helped the team refocus and meet the deadline.

### Difficulty
Advanced

### Pronunciation
/səˈɡeɪʃəs/

### Synonymn
wise, prudent, shrewd, astute

### Antonymn
foolish, unwise, naïve, short-sighted
