# Word of the Day

## plausible

### Meaning
Seeming reasonable or probable; appearing worthy of belief.

### Example
She gave a plausible explanation for why she was late, and everyone accepted it without question.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ˈplɔːzɪbəl/

### Synonymn
credible, believable, reasonable, likely

### Antonymn
implausible, unlikely, doubtful, incredible
