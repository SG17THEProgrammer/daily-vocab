# Word of the Day

## serendipity

### Meaning
the occurrence of finding something valuable or delightful when you are not looking for it

### Example
I was browsing through a vintage bookstore and stumbled upon a rare edition of my favorite novel, it was a wonderful serendipity

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
/ˌsɛrəndɪˈpɪtɪ/

### Synonym
luck
