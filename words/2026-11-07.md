# Word of the Day

## indefatigable

### Meaning
Showing sustained energy and determination; unable to be tired out.

### Example
Despite the long hours of campaigning, her indefatigable spirit kept the volunteers motivated.

### Difficulty
Advanced

### Pronunciation
/ɪnˌdɛf.əˈtɪɡ.ə.bəl/

### Synonymn
tireless, unflagging, persistent, untiring

### Antonymn
fatigable, listless, lethargic, indolent
