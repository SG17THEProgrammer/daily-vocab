# Word of the Day

## sagacious

### Meaning
Having or showing keen mental discernment, good judgment, and wisdom.

### Example
The sagacious manager anticipated the market shift and adjusted the company's strategy before anyone else noticed.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
səˈɡeɪʃəs

### Synonymn
wise, insightful, shrewd, astute, perceptive

### Antonymn
foolish, ignorant, imprudent, short-sighted
