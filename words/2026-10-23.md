# Word of the Day

## ostentatious

### Meaning
characterized by vulgar or pretentious display; designed to attract notice and impress others

### Example
He bought an ostentatious sports car just to show off his success at the weekend party.

### Difficulty
advanced

### Pronunciation
os-TEN-tay-shus

### Synonymn
flamboyant, pretentious, showy, garish

### Antonymn
modest, unassuming, subdued, plain
