# Word of the Day

## intrepid

### Meaning
Showing fearless courage and a willingness to take bold risks.

### Example
The intrepid explorer trekked through the uncharted jungle, undeterred by the harsh conditions.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ɪnˈtrɛpɪd/

### Synonymn
bold, courageous, daring, valiant

### Antonymn
timid, fearful, cowardly, cautious
