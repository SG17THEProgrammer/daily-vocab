# Word of the Day

## audacious

### Meaning
Showing a willingness to take bold risks; daring, often in a way that shocks or provokes.

### Example
She made an audacious move by quitting her stable job to start her own bakery.

### Difficulty
Upper-intermediate

### Pronunciation
ˈɔː.dɪ.ʃəs

### Synonymn
bold, daring, fearless, intrepid

### Antonymn
timid, cautious, cowardly, meek
