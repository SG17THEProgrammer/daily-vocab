# Word of the Day

## placid

### Meaning
Not easily upset or excited; calm and peaceful.

### Example
Even when the traffic jam stretched for miles, she remained placid, humming softly to herself.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
ˈplæsɪd

### Synonymn
calm, serene, tranquil, composed

### Antonymn
agitated, turbulent, restless, volatile
