# Word of the Day

## conspicuous

### Meaning
Easily seen or noticed; standing out so that it attracts attention.

### Example
She wore a bright red coat that made her conspicuous in the crowd of people wearing muted colors.

### Difficulty
intermediate

### Pronunciation
/kənˈspɪkjuəs/

### Synonymn
noticeable, prominent, evident, obvious

### Antonymn
inconspicuous, hidden, obscure, unnoticeable
