# Word of the Day

## verbose

### Meaning
Using more words than necessary; overly wordy.

### Example
The professor's lecture was so verbose that half the class started drifting off.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
/vɜːrˈboʊs/

### Synonymn
wordy, long-winded, prolix, rambling

### Antonymn
concise, succinct, terse, laconic
