# Word of the Day

## astute

### Meaning
Having or showing an ability to accurately assess situations or people and turn this to one's advantage; shrewd.

### Example
She made an astute observation about the market trend, which helped the team adjust their strategy quickly.

### Difficulty
Intermediate

### Pronunciation
/ˈæs.tjuːt/

### Synonymn
shrewd, perceptive, insightful, clever

### Antonymn
naïve, clueless, ignorant, obtuse
